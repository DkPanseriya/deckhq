The record

The engineering log is DEVIATIONS.md rendered: every place the build departed from its own blueprint, with the reason and the measurement, written as it goes rather than afterwards. It contains the budgets that were missed and the claims that did not survive being run on a machine, because those are the entries worth having.

CHANGELOG.md is what changed and when. plan/08-PLAN-V2-100X.md is what happens next, work package by work package, including the ones that are only planned.
