The look.

Three finishes ship today, all free and none of them gated. Each one repaints materials and neutrals; the six state colours never move, so a raised hand is the same amber in all three.

Pick one in Settings, or type Look: Night lab into the command palette. Nothing is behind a purchase and nothing is behind an account.

The same room, three times.

Ground, wood, fabric and light change. The plate stays legible, every ink keeps its contrast floor, and the working green on the agents is the same green in all three.

Furnish it yourself.

⌘K → Settings → Look is where the building gets its materials. Six presets to start from, then a floor material per zone, a colour scheme, a furniture set, rugs, planting and the lounge kit.

  • Presets, then the details

    Studio oak, Night lab, Paper office, Terrazzo hall, Garden floor, Workshop. Each preset card is a real thumbnail of your floor, painted by the thing that paints the floor, so what you pick is what you get.

  • Agent size, and the furniture follows

    Small, medium, large, or automatic. Five agents can fill the window and a hundred still fit, and the desks, chairs, sofas and rugs resize with the people rather than staying put.

  • You cannot make it unreadable

    A combination that would leave a label or a rug too faint to read is refused, the reason appears beside the control that caused it, and nothing changes until you pick something that works.

  • A floor you can post

    deckhq look export writes your interior to a file that names no project, no path and no session, so it is safe to hand to somebody else. deckhq look import puts theirs on.

Packs

Extra themes and avatar sets arrive as a single file. deckhq pack install <file> copies it into ~/.deckhq/packs/ and a running DeckHQ picks it up within a second. A pack carries colours and names, and there is nowhere in its format to put a tier, a licence, an expiry or a switch that turns a feature off.

Every theme in a pack is held to the same readability floor as a theme DeckHQ ships, and every avatar colour is kept clear of all six state colours, so an agent can never be mistaken for a state. No account, no network call.
